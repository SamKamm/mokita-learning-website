How It Works

What working with us actually looks like.

No long proposals, no off-the-shelf solutions. We start by listening.

01

A conversation — no strings attached

We start with a free call, usually 30–45 minutes. You tell us what's going on. We ask questions. No pitch, no proposal yet — just an honest conversation to see whether we're a good fit and whether we can genuinely help.

02

We listen before we design

If there's a fit, we spend time properly understanding your organisation before we suggest anything. This might involve speaking with a few people, observing a team meeting, or reviewing existing materials. We won't design something without understanding the context it's going into.

03

A proposal built around you

We put together a clear proposal — what we'd do, why, how long it would take, and what it costs. Nothing vague. You'll know exactly what you're getting.

04

The work

We deliver — whether that's a single workshop, a coaching programme, or a longer learning journey. We stay flexible as things evolve, because organisations are living things and plans change.

05

Reflection and what's next

At the end of any engagement we take stock together. What changed? What didn't? What would be useful next? Some clients work with us once. Others come back repeatedly as their organisations grow and change.
