What We Do

Real work, for real organisations.

Every engagement is built around what your team actually needs — not a generic programme delivered off a shelf.

Workshops

Our workshops create space for teams to slow down, think clearly, and talk honestly — often about things that have been avoided for a long time. We design each session around your specific context, using approaches drawn from psychology, philosophy, and organisational development.

Topics we regularly work on include:

  • Psychological safety and trust
  • Leadership under pressure
  • Difficult conversations
  • Team dynamics and conflict
  • Burnout, resilience, and wellbeing at work
  • Organisational values — what they really mean

Sessions run from a half-day to two days. We can work with teams of any size.

Coaching

One-to-one coaching for leaders and managers who want to think more clearly, lead more effectively, and handle the complexity of their role with greater confidence.

This isn't advice-giving or mentoring. It's a structured, confidential space to explore what's really going on — and to develop your own thinking rather than borrowing someone else's answers.

We also offer team coaching, working with groups to improve how they function together — particularly useful when a team is going through change, conflict, or a shift in leadership.

Learning Programs

For organisations that want lasting change rather than a one-off event, we design and deliver multi-session learning journeys. These typically run over three to six months and combine workshops, coaching, peer learning, and reflection.

Past programs have focused on:

  • Building a culture of psychological safety across a whole organisation
  • Developing middle managers as the engine of culture change
  • Supporting staff through organisational restructure
  • Leadership development for senior teams

Consultancy

Sometimes you need a thinking partner before you know what you need. We offer initial consultancy sessions to help organisations diagnose what's really going on, identify where to focus, and design an approach that fits.

This is often where longer engagements begin.

Not sure what you need?
